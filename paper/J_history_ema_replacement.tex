\subsection{History-EMA Normalized Update Deviation}
\label{subsec:history-ema-j}

Let $\Delta^G_t$ denote the realized final Cloud model update at round $t$.
The global reference is the EMA of historical Cloud updates that satisfy the
method's upload-boundary eligibility conditions:
\begin{equation}
\bar{\Delta}^{G}_{t}
=\rho\bar{\Delta}^{G}_{t-1}+(1-\rho)\Delta^{G}_{t},
\label{eq:history-global-ema}
\end{equation}
where $\rho\in[0,1)$ is the EMA decay. For each Cloud-reaching Mode
$m\in\{\mathrm{LIIEIIIC},\mathrm{LIIC}\}$, let $\bar u_{m,t}$ be the EMA of
its sample-weighted mean Cloud submission-unit update. The mean is computed
from actual submission units; for LIIEIIIC, all clients assigned to the same
Edge are first treated as one joint Edge-to-Cloud submission unit.

For a candidate profile $h$, let $\mathcal C_t(h)$ be its Cloud-reaching
submission units, and let $n_i$ be the sample count represented by unit $i$.
The Cloud aggregation weight is
\begin{equation}
I_i(h)=\frac{n_i}{\sum_{j\in\mathcal C_t(h)}n_j}.
\label{eq:history-cloud-sample-weights}
\end{equation}
For each Mode $m$, define its candidate sample-mass fraction
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{m,t}(h)=\sum_{i\in\mathcal C_t(h):m_i=m}I_i(h),
\qquad \sum_m\alpha_{m,t}(h)=1.
\label{eq:history-mode-mass}
\end{equation}
The pre-decision prediction of the candidate Cloud update is
\begin{equation}
\widehat{\Delta}^{C}_{t}(h)=
\sum_{m\in\{\mathrm{LIIEIIIC},\mathrm{LIIC}\}}
\alpha_{m,t}(h)\bar u_{m,t-1}.
\label{eq:history-predicted-cloud-update}
\end{equation}
All $I_i$ are defined against the complete candidate Cloud submission set;
weights are not renormalized separately per Mode. The Edge-loop count $E_B$
describes execution and is not a contribution multiplier.

The proposed score compares the predicted candidate Cloud update with the
historical global-update EMA:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{
J_t(h)=
\frac{\left\|\widehat{\Delta}^{C}_{t}(h)-\bar{\Delta}^{G}_{t-1}\right\|_2^2}
{\left\|\bar{\Delta}^{G}_{t-1}\right\|_2^2+\varepsilon}
}
\label{eq:history-ema-j}
\end{equation}
where $\varepsilon>0$ prevents division by zero. The selector uses $J_t(h)$
after the global history and both Cloud-reaching Mode histories are available.
During warm-up, when those histories are incomplete, the selector assigns the
same neutral $J$ score to every profile and relies on the other feasibility and
latency objectives; it does not fabricate a missing update or read a certificate.

LIIE is Edge-only in the current implementation and does not submit an update
to Cloud. Its Cloud contribution is therefore zero rather than a fabricated
submission. LIIC protects $L_C^{\mathrm{upd}}$ before Cloud aggregation;
LIIEIIIC protects $L_E^{\mathrm{upd}}$ before Edge aggregation and does not
protect $E_C^{\mathrm{upd}}$. HE and aggregate-DP are outside this method and
are disabled. $J_t$ is an update-deviation objective; by itself it does not
prove a monotonic relationship with test accuracy or constitute a learning-error
bound.
